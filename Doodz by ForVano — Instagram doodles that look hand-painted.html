FAQ

Good to know

Everything worth knowing before your first doodle.

What is Doodz?

Doodz is an Android app that automatically draws your chosen image as a doodle inside Instagram. You pick the picture and the area, and the app handles the taps, colours and strokes for you.

What is the difference between the modes?

Standard draws a clean single-colour doodle quickly: upload the image, select the area, select the brush and tap Start. There's no colour setup.

Hyper Doodle takes more time and builds a detailed multicolour doodle using Instagram's colour palettes. It adds a short 2-dot calibration so the app knows where the colour picker and brush rest point are on your screen.

Which mode should I start with?

Start with Standard. It's the fastest way to see how Doodz works. Move to Hyper Doodle when you want a detailed multicolour result and don't mind it taking longer.

How long does a doodle take?

Standard is the quickest mode. Hyper Doodle is slower because it swaps colours and draws more lines, so the time depends on how detailed your image is and how big the drawing area is. The preview shows the line count before you start, which gives you a good idea of what to expect.

Which images work best?

Clear, well-defined images with strong shapes and contrast tend to turn out best, such as logos, portraits and bold artwork. Very busy or tiny-detail photos have to be simplified into lines and a limited palette, so they won't look identical to the original.

Will the colours match my image exactly?

Hyper Doodle can only use the colours Instagram's doodle tool offers, so each part of your image is matched to the closest available colour. It stays as true to the original as the palette permits, but expect a palette-based look rather than a perfect photo copy.

Can I check the result before it draws?

Yes. The preview shows colours, size and line count before the first stroke, so you can check everything before anything is drawn.

The drawing looks misaligned. What should I do?

Check the position of your drawing area first. In Hyper Doodle, redo the 2-dot calibration: dot 1 on the colour picker, and dot 2 on the brush's rest point in the middle of the screen (visible once the picker is selected). Then deselect the picker before starting.

Which permissions does it need?

Only the Accessibility permission. It lets the app perform the taps and swipes that draw for you. Nothing else is required.

Do my images leave my phone?

No. Images and screenshots are analysed and the drawing plan is created on your device.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. Doodz depends on Android accessibility and overlay features. iOS is on the wish list.

Is it Instagram only?

For now, yes. Snapchat and WhatsApp are planned next.

Is it affiliated with Instagram?

No. Instagram is a trademark of Meta, and Doodz is not endorsed by Meta. Please use it responsibly.
