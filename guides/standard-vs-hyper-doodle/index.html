Standard vs Hyper Doodle: which mode should you use?

Quick answer: start with Standard for a fast, clean single-colour doodle. Choose Hyper Doodle when you want a detailed multicolour result and don't mind it taking longer.

StandardHyper Doodle
ColoursSingle colourMulticolour, matched to Instagram's palette
SpeedQuickestSlower
SetupUpload image, select area, select brush, tap StartSelect area, calibrate with 2 dots, tap Start
CalibrationNone2 dots
Best forFast, good-looking resultsDetailed masterpieces

When to choose Standard

You want it done quickly, your image is simple (a logo, text or bold shape), or you're trying Doodz for the first time.

When to choose Hyper Doodle

Your image has several colours and detail, such as a portrait or cartoon, and you want it to stay as true to the original as Instagram's palette allows.
