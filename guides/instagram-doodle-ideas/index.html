Instagram doodle ideas to auto-draw with Doodz

Quick answer: bold, high-contrast images work best. Try portraits, logos, memes, anime or cartoon characters, birthday wishes, couple pictures and short quotes.

Ideas worth trying

  • Portraits: a friend's face as a hand-painted-looking doodle.
  • Logos: your brand or channel logo drawn live in the chat.
  • Memes: send a meme as a doodle for extra impact.
  • Anime and cartoons: flat colours and clear outlines suit Hyper Doodle.
  • Birthday wishes and greetings: a custom picture for the occasion.
  • Couple pics: a personal doodle for someone special.
  • Quotes and text: simple shapes draw fast in Standard mode.
